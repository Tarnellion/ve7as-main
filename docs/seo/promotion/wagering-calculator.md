## Linkworthy angle (corrected against live competitors)

Verified live: [ve7as.com/en/tools/wagering-calculator/](https://ve7as.com/en/tools/wagering-calculator/) (outputs include, quoted: "Required turnover", "Actual turnover after weighting", "Expected loss on the turnover — Turnover × (100% − RTP)", framed as "the average price of clearing", with the disclaimer "The calculator shows mathematical expectation, not a prediction of your session"). Competitors: [americancasinoguide.com wagering calculator](https://www.americancasinoguide.com/bonuses/wagering-calculator) (live, fetched) and [casimonka.com](https://casimonka.com/utilities/bonus-wagering-requirements-calculator/) (live, fetched). bonus.com and playusa.com/tools 403'd to automated fetch — **do not cite them as verified comparisons** until checked in a normal browser.

1. **It prices the bonus as a cost; competitors either stop at turnover or frame RTP as winnings.** Correction from the original plan: it is NOT true that every competitor stops at turnover. Casimonka verifiably does ("Amount to Wager" only, 100% contribution assumed). But American Casino Guide's calculator does use game RTP — its output is a "Theoretical Win" figure. The honest differentiator: ACG frames the RTP math as what you might *win*; ours frames the same math as what clearing will *cost* ("expected loss exceeds the bonus ⇒ clearing is unprofitable on average"). Cost-framing plus per-game weighting plus the explicit −EV verdict is the unique angle. Pitch line: "the calculator that tells you the price of the bonus, not the size of the chore or a theoretical win."
2. **It is safe to cite.** Verified: both live competitor calculators sit on affiliate sites (ACG links 888 Casino, Golden Nugget etc.; Casimonka carries "Visit" CTAs to casinos). Ve7as verifiably has none: no operator ratings, no affiliate links, no ads, no sign-up, client-side only, "Independent information portal… Not a gambling operator" on-page. "The only wagering calculator you can link without sending your readers to a casino" survives review — this is now the *lead* differentiator, since it holds against every competitor including ACG.
3. **Insider-built, cost-framed.** Built by a QA/SDET who tests a casino platform for a living; the framing matches harm-reduction messaging. Always told with the "shows the cost of gambling, not ways to win" caveat attached. This is the E-E-A-T story for outreach and LinkedIn.

Known scope limit to concede proactively in math-literate venues: the closed form (turnover × house edge) is the *average* cost and ignores variance, max-bet rules and win caps. Where those bind, simulation beats the formula. Conceding this up front is what separates a contribution from a pitch — see the Substack item.

- **Never post the tool in recovery spaces** — r/problemgambling, GamCare, Gambling Therapy, any support community. A "what your bonus really costs" calculator aimed at people in recovery is harm, not harm-reduction, and such communities ban promotion anyway. RG *educators* may get an email; RG *support spaces* get nothing.
- **No Reddit activity until rules are read in-app and quoted.** No sockpuppets, no reposting the same comment across subs, ever.
- **On WoV:** no links in the first 30 days; no thread about the tool without prior mod permission (rule quoted above); never the same message in two places (rule quoted above); and **do not link the calculator into threads that are only adjacent to it** (the "Casino Bonus Abusing" lesson — the OP asked a different question).
- **On CHW/advantage-play venues:** methodology and cost language only; never wording that reads as bonus-profit coaching.
- **On GPWA:** no promotion in threads — explicitly reserved to sponsor members. Signature/profile only, after status.
- **On Product Hunt:** no upvote solicitation, no relaunch games.
- **Do not submit to AlternativeTo** (FAQ explicitly declines calculators); no bulk directory submissions; no citing bonus.com/playusa as verified comparisons until manually checked.
- **Everywhere:** disclose who built it ("I made this"), never frame results as a way to win, never name or recommend operators, and if a moderator says no — that venue is closed, no workarounds.
