---
question: "Is it safe to gamble online?"
---

Gambling always carries the risk of losing money — that's part of its nature,
not a "problem with a particular platform." If you do decide to take part, do
so only on licensed platforms, set a budget in advance that you can afford to
lose, and use the limits operators offer. For more on the criteria for
choosing a reliable platform, see our guide in the "Casinos and slots"
section.
