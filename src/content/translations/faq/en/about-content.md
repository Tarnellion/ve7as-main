question: "What is this site about, and why doesn't it have casino rankings?"
---

Ve7as is an independent informational portal about gambling and betting
entertainment: casinos and slots, sports betting, poker and table games,
lotteries, and esports betting. We deliberately don't publish rankings of
specific operators or place affiliate links — our goal is to give readers
objective context, not to push them toward signing up on any particular
platform.
