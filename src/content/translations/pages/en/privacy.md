This page describes what data Ve7as collects and how it's used.

## What data we collect

We may collect anonymized visit statistics (pages viewed, device, approximate
location based on IP address) using analytics tools — this helps us
understand which material readers find interesting.

If you write to us by email, we receive your address and the content of your
message — this data is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

## How we use the data

We don't sell or share personal data with third parties, except where
required by law. Analytics data is used in aggregate form to improve the
site's content.

## Cookies

The site may use technical and analytics cookies to function correctly and
to collect anonymized statistics. You can manage cookies through your
browser settings.

## Contact us

For questions about data processing, write to hello@ve7as.com.
