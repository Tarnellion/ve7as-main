title: "Bankroll management: the basic rules"
description: How to allocate your budget wisely, set limits, and stay disciplined — even when you're not always up on money.
---

A bankroll is the amount of money you consciously set aside for betting or
gaming and are prepared to lose without harming your overall budget. Bankroll
management is the core skill that separates mindful leisure from impulsive
spending.

## Basic rules

1. **Define your bankroll in advance.** It should be an amount whose absence
   won't affect your bills or everyday life.
2. **Stake a fixed percentage.** Experienced players rarely risk more than
   1–5% of their bankroll on a single event or session.
3. **Keep records.** A simple spreadsheet of bets and results quickly shows
   whether your chosen strategy is working or needs to be revisited.
4. **Set limits on the platform.** Most licensed operators let you set a
   daily or weekly deposit limit — use this option before you start playing,
   not after a big loss.

## Allocation strategies

- **Flat betting** — a fixed amount on every event, the most predictable
  option for beginners.
- **Percentage betting** — the stake size is recalculated from your current
  bankroll, which naturally reduces risk during a downswing.
- **The Kelly criterion** — a mathematical formula for sizing bets based on
  your estimated edge; it requires accurate probability estimates and
  discipline, and suits experienced players.

## The bottom line

Bankroll management doesn't guarantee a win — it guarantees that the activity
stays a form of entertainment rather than a source of financial trouble.
