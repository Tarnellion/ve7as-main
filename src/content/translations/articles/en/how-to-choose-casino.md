Choosing a casino is, first and foremost, a question of the safety of your
money and the fairness of the games. Here are seven criteria worth checking
before you sign up on a new platform.

## 1. Licensing

MGA (Malta) and UKGC (United Kingdom) licenses are considered the strictest:
they require casinos to undergo regular RTP audits and keep players' funds
separate from operating funds. Curaçao eGaming is a softer option, common
among newer platforms.

## 2. Payout speed

A withdrawal processed within 24–48 hours for verified accounts is considered
a good benchmark. If reviews frequently mention delays, that's a red flag.

## 3. RTP integrity

Reputable providers (Pragmatic Play, NetEnt, Evolution, Play'n GO) publish the
theoretical RTP of each game and undergo independent audits by labs such as
eCOGRA or iTech Labs.

## 4. Bonus terms

Pay attention to the wagering requirement size, maximum bet limits, and the
list of games that count toward the wagering.

## 5. Payment methods

Check whether the platform supports the payment methods you're used to —
cards, e-wallets, cryptocurrency — and whether there are hidden fees on
withdrawals.

## 6. Support

Round-the-clock chat with response times of just a few minutes is a sign that
the platform invests in service, not just advertising.

## 7. Reviews from real players

Read reviews on independent sites and forums, paying attention not to emotion
but to recurring facts — especially complaints about non-payment.
