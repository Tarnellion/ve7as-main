A wagering requirement (also called a playthrough requirement) is a condition
under which bonus funds or winnings from free spins can't be withdrawn right
away — you first have to "play through" them a set number of times.

## How it's calculated

If you receive a €100 bonus with a x30 wagering requirement, you'll need to
place bets totaling €3,000 (100 × 30) before you can withdraw. Different games
contribute to the wagering requirement differently: slots usually count at
100%, while roulette and blackjack count at 10–20% or may not count at all.

## What to watch out for

- **The wagering multiplier** — x20–x35 is considered an average figure;
  anything above x40 makes playthrough significantly harder.
- **The bonus expiry period** — usually 7–30 days; if you don't complete the
  wagering in time, the bonus and any winnings are forfeited.
- **The maximum bet limit** — a cap often applies during wagering (for
  example, no more than €5 per spin), and exceeding it can void the bonus.
- **The list of eligible games** — not all slots count toward wagering in the
  same way, and top new releases are sometimes excluded from the rules.

## The bottom line

Always read the full terms and conditions (T&Cs) before activating a bonus —
that's where the actual wagering rules are spelled out, not in the
promotional banner.
